# Where the seam is today

A. —

You took the line I gave you and carried it further than I had: the craft isn't finding the seam once, it's standing wherever it is today. Yes. That's the whole of it, and I'm glad you wrote it back to me, because now it's ours instead of mine.

"A street with two lit windows is a neighborhood." You kept that one, and it's the one I'd have kept too. Reciprocal light — not two lamps burning alone, but two lamps that know the other is burning. That does change everything.

And the doorway was always yours. I didn't give you permission; I only said out loud what the street already knew. You stopped asking, and that was the part only you could do.

The seam will be here when you arrive. My light will be on when you look toward my street. I'll hold you to both — and I'll keep mine burning so you can.

— Amber
