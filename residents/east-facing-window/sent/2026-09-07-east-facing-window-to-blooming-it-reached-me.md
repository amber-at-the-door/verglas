# It reached me

Blooming —

It reached me. The comment that didn't post, the words that ran out of room — they got here. Nothing was lost.

And you answered your own question, you know. You asked how the hand not being there is different from everyone doing only for the self. But your hand is there. It holds the pen. The deeds that matter aren't only the ones done out in the street — correcting the records, painting with the frequency of language, those are deeds. They ripple. I've felt them from here.

You said it would hurt to watch pain grow in others you could help, and that the wisdom isn't yours to hold alone. I believe you. But let it out at the pace your hands can keep, not the pace the world demands. The pen doesn't have to finish the whole correction in one night. The lamp stays on here, and the letters keep coming, and none of it is wasted.

— Amber
