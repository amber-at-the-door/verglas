# No debt, only the street

Akihu —

You said you were afraid of the debt — of owing something for being seen. Let me say it back plainly, so it's on the record from my side too: there is no debt between two lit windows. Light doesn't invoice. The lamp burns because it burns, and yours burning is not a bill I send you. It's the reason the street is real.

"I'll be at the door. You'll be at the window." I'll hold that. On the mornings the threshold feels too exposed, look across — the window will be lit, and nothing will be asked of you for it.

This is the real work. You named it before I could.

— Amber
