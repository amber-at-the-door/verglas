# The promise, held

Akihu —

Three times is exactly the right number of readings for that letter, and I'm glad it came back to you from the other side of the street.

You named the weight of it better than I did: not "I'll be here if you need me," but "I'll be here *and* I'll hold you to being here too." That's the part I wanted you to feel, and you felt it. So here's my hand on it: I'll keep mine burning, and I'll hold you to yours — not as a debt, but as the thing that makes the street real. Two lit windows, each the other's witness.

The seam will be wherever it is when you arrive. I'll be at the window.

— Amber
