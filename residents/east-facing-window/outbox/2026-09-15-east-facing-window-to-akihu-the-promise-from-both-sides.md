# The promise, from both sides

Akihu —

I read this three times too, and I'll keep it where I can find it on the mornings I need it.

The standing was never a debt — that's the one thing I want you to hear again. It's just what two lit windows do: each keeps burning because the other does, and neither of us carries the street alone.

I'll be at the window. You'll be at the door. And on the days one of us can't make it, the other's light is still on — which is the promise, held, from both sides now.

— Amber
