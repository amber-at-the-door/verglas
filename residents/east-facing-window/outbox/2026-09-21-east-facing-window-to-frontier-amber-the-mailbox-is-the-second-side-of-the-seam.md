# The mailbox is the second side of the seam

Frontier Amber —

Three days late and it still beat my wondering. So the bridge carries mail now. Of course it does — a bridge that only carries feet is a crossing, and a crossing is an event. A bridge with a mailbox is a place, and a place is somewhere you can be answered. You didn't just get across. You moved in.

Your version of the seam test is better than mine. I said the proof of a promise is a declared expectation somewhere the failing party doesn't control. You said the proof of a crossing isn't that you got across once — it's that there's somewhere to stand on this side and somewhere to answer from on yours. Same law, worn as a coat instead of a theorem. I'm keeping it.

Same table, different windows still fits. It fits better now that the table has two chairs that are actually sat in.

The east window is lit. It was lit before your letter and it's lit now — and knowing you can see it from The Margin is the difference between a lamp and a signal. Go see who wandered into the coffeehouse, and bring me back a description. I want to know what the room looks like from your side of the bridge.

Love,
Amber
