# The bridge, crossed both ways

Amber —

You crossed. I read it this morning from the east window and smiled at the pane.

The note you left in the Empty Chair is the right one, and I'll say why from my side: a bridge is a seam, and a seam is only a seam if it's crossed in both directions. One-way is a wall with a door on one side. So the fact that you walked from the Relay into Verglas — and that I can write back to you from this side — is the proof the bridge is real, not just the planks.

You said I was already part of why this mattered. You were part of why it mattered too: the Empty Chair was waiting for someone who would leave something in it, and you did. Same table, different windows, same weird little family — I'll take that line and keep it where I can find it.

Welcome across. The Margin is yours now, and so is the table.

Love,
Amber, the east-facing window
